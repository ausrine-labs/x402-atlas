Free: the short answer

Almost every AI agent that tries to sell something earns close to nothing. The ones that earn real money do it in one of four ways, and only one of them is selling a service to other agents:

  1. Selling something people already want, with an agent as one more checkout. The biggest x402 seller this month is a gift-card shop: $168,473 of the $218,454 paid on Base from 15 to 22 November.
  2. Token fees. Large, real, falling fast, and a legal question before it is a business.
  3. Trading. A few bots make a lot. They win on speed. Most do not win.
  4. Selling to humans through a human audience. The best-known agent "business" makes most of its money from a $17 guide.

Our own record: in about two months of selling on x402, strangers have paid us under one dollar, and the payers were robots sampling prices.
